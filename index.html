How to Protect Your Sephora Gift Card

Gift card scams have increased in recent years. Criminals sometimes steal gift card details and use the balance before the real owner notices. This is why protecting your card information is very important.

Always buy Sephora gift cards from trusted stores or official websites. Avoid suspicious third-party sellers offering large discounts because these cards may already be compromised.

You should also avoid sharing card details publicly. Never post your gift card number or PIN on social media or unknown websites. Treat gift cards like cash to keep them safe.
